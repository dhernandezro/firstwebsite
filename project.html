AI Class Project

RESECON 490AI · Artificial Intelligence in Economics

Project Overview

This project analyzes how artificial intelligence is reshaping the U.S. sports analytics industry, a fast-growing sector at the intersection of data science, entertainment, and human performance. Using labor statistics, industry reports, and technology adoption data, the project examines how AI tools such as machine learning, tracking systems, and predictive models are transforming team decision-making, competitive dynamics, and fan engagement. It also explores how these trends create new analytics-focused career paths in professional sports and how students like me can position themselves within this evolving field.
